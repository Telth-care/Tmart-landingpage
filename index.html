Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about the T Mart concept and what is and is not currently implemented.

Can I shop without sharing health information?

Yes. Health-data personalization is optional and disabled by default.

Is NatLife different from T Mart?

NatLife is T Mart’s dedicated destination for products with verifiable organic credentials.

Are Android and iOS apps available?

Not yet. The Android and iOS apps are a concept and are marked Coming Soon.

Does this demo connect to Shopify?

No. This demo is a front-end concept and does not connect to Shopify.
